Dodge ’Em Overdrive

How to play

  1. This is you. Your car drives itself, counter-clockwise.
  2. Cyan cars drive the other way. Never meet one in your lane.
  3. You switch lanes at the glowing doors in the walls.
  4. Grab gold gems for points. Hold for an Overdrive boost.
  5. Power-ups float above the track — Shield, Magnet, Slow-mo, Double. Drive through to grab one.
